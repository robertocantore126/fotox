# M6-T03 — Crop tool

Agent: Codebuff (core, tool, first UI), finished by Claude Opus 5.5  ·  Branch: `m6`  ·  Status: ready for review

## What was done

The work was started by the other agent (Codebuff), which stopped before
committing it. Claude reviewed that code, finished it and fixed what follows.

- `Command::Crop { rect, angle_deg, delete_cropped }` (fx-core): with no angle
  and no deletion it is Canvas Size (offsets only, no pixel rewritten,
  D-056); `delete_cropped` clips every pixel layer, mask and the selection to
  the rectangle first (`pixels::clip_to_rect`: tiles outside go, crossed
  tiles are cleared outside, tiles inside keep their handle); an angle
  resamples every image through the turn about the box's centre into the
  box's frame (T01's resampler, `resample_document`). Runs as a pixel job.
- `pixels::content_bounds` (the exact content rectangle, tile-parallel,
  uniform tiles answered by one value): Image ▸ Crop (`img:crop`) crops to the
  selection's coverage; Image ▸ Trim (`edit:trim`) crops to the active
  layer's content, based on transparency or a corner colour, on the ticked
  edges only.
- Crop tool (`tools/crop.rs`): the box starts as the canvas, 8 handles,
  rule-of-thirds grid, the outside dimmed 50 %; Shift keeps the box's ratio,
  Alt resizes about the centre, the option bar's Ratio / W / H constrain it;
  a press inside moves the box, a press in a 28-px ring outside turns it
  (Shift = 15° steps), a press farther out draws a new box. Enter or ✓ sends
  one `Crop` (one History step); Escape or ✗ puts the box back.
- Option bar: Ratio, W, H, Delete Cropped Pixels (off by default, D-056), ✓
  and ✗ (a button group whose buttons are actions: `tool:commit`,
  `tool:cancel`). Image ▸ Crop is enabled; the Trim dialog's choices are sent
  to the engine.

### Claude's review fixes

- **The straighten was invisible.** The box stayed upright on screen while the
  angle changed, so the user could not see what they were straightening to.
  The box now turns with the pointer (a `turn_deg` of its own); handles, grid
  and shade follow it, hit tests and resizes work in the box's own frame, and
  the command's angle is the opposite of the box's turn (a box turned to
  follow a horizon that leans 10° crops an image turned by −10°: level).
- **The shade had to be a convex polygon**: `OverlayItem::Shade` takes four
  corners now, and `tessellate` fills the viewport outside any convex
  quadrilateral (clipped to the viewport, then angular sectors about its
  centroid). M6-T05's rotated view needs the same.
- **The box went stale.** It was reset right after the tool sent its
  command, but Crop is a job: the box was reset to the *old* canvas. The
  engine now hands the active tool the document again when the canvas size
  changes (the job finishing, an undo across a crop, Canvas Size…) and when
  another document becomes active (`reactivate_tool`).
- **Masks moved away from their layer (T02 and T03).** A linked mask sits at
  its layer's offset and every other mask at the canvas origin
  (`fx_render::program`), but the geometry commands treated every mask as
  canvas coverage at (0, 0): after moving a layer that has a mask, a quarter
  turn, Image Size, Rotate Arbitrary or a straighten put the mask somewhere
  else than its layer, and Canvas Size / Crop left an unlinked mask (or the
  mask of a group or adjustment layer) where it was while the canvas moved.
  Now each mask is transformed where it sits (`mask_origin`) and put where it
  must sit afterwards with the new `pixels::place_at` (exact; a whole-tile
  shift moves tiles, otherwise tiles are reassembled; the uncovered band is
  the mask's outside value). Tests: `a_linked_mask_of_a_moved_layer_turns_with_its_layer`,
  `an_unlinked_mask_moves_with_the_canvas`, `place_at_keeps_every_canvas_pixel_where_it_was`.
  Limitation: a mask has no offset, so what an origin mask loses past the
  new origin (a crop from the left) does not come back if the canvas grows
  again.
- **The fx-render test binary could hang** (seen twice on 25 Sep, ~900 MB, no
  progress for 30 minutes): its dozen GPU tests each create a device with the
  adapter's full limits, all in parallel. They now take one lock
  (`testing::one_gpu_test`), as the engine's integration harness already
  does.
- Trim's "Trim Away" starts with all four edges ticked (Photoshop), not only
  Top.
- Whitespace damage in `actions.js`, `options.js` and `optionsbar.js` (lines
  joined with tabs) repaired.
- `docs/PROTOCOL.md`: the M6 actions and the crop command.
