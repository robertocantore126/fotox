# M6-T04 — Free Transform and Warp

Agent: Claude Opus 5.5  ·  Branch: `m6`  ·  Status: ready for review

## Plan (written before coding)

- The geometry in fx-core (`Mapping::from_quad`), the command
  (`Command::Transform`) resampling through T01, a box that never touches the
  document, a preview on the visible tiles only, and one history step.
- The box is not a toolbar tool: like Photoshop's, it is a mode over any
  tool. The engine holds the session and routes the pointer and keys to it.

## What was done

- **fx-core**
  - `Mapping::from_quad(rect, quad)`: a parallelogram gives an affine
    mapping (scale, rotate, skew), any other convex quad a homography
    (Heckbert's square-to-quad composed with rect → unit square).
  - `quad_is_convex`: a folded or collapsed box is refused.
  - `dest_rect` boxes a warp by its control points (convex hull property).
  - `Command::Transform { layer, mapping: Box<Mapping>, filter }` ("Free
    Transform"):
    - Without a selection, the layer's content (cut to its non-empty tiles
      first, so a small object on a canvas-sized layer resamples only its
      box) and its linked mask are resampled.
    - With a selection, the selected pixels are lifted (cut to the
      selection's tile bounds), the hole is cleared, the lifted pixels are
      transformed and composited back (`pixels::over`), and the selection
      moves with them. It is one history step.
    - The layer must be a pixel layer, unlocked (position and pixels).
    - It runs as a pixel job.
  - `pixels::place_in` (a sized `place_at`), `pixels::over`.
- **fx-ops**: a destination tile whose source tiles are all empty is
  answered at once (the corners of a turned image, the space around a small
  object).
- **fx-engine**
  - `tools/transform.rs`, the box (`Session`), with Photoshop 2019+'s
    gestures. The box's own axes are used throughout, so a turned or skewed
    box scales along its sides:
    - a corner scales proportionally (Shift frees it, Alt scales about the
      reference point), a side scales one axis (Shift both);
    - inside moves, outside rotates (Shift: 15° steps);
    - the reference point can be dragged;
    - Ctrl + corner distorts, Ctrl + side moves the side, Ctrl+Shift + side
      skews (Alt: symmetric), Ctrl+Alt+Shift + corner is perspective;
    - the submenu's modes make one gesture the default;
    - Warp starts from the box's shape: drag a control point, or drag inside
      the surface to pull the points by their weight there;
    - the arrows nudge (Shift ×10), Enter commits, Escape cancels.
  - `transform_preview.rs`:
    - A preparation job cuts out the source (content, or lifted selection +
      hole) and makes every mip level valid once.
    - Each update resamples only the **visible** tiles at the view level
      (one level coarser while dragging, refined 150 ms after the pointer
      rests), on rayon; the latest request wins.
    - The render snapshot shows the result in place of the layer, or the
      layer with its hole plus a floating layer above it.
    - A committed transform stays on screen until its job's pixels land.
  - `engine.rs`: the `xf:*` actions.
    - `xf:free` (Ctrl+T) and `xf:scale` … `xf:warp` start a box or switch its
      mode.
    - `xf:rot180` / `xf:rot90cw` / `xf:rot90ccw` / `xf:flip-h` / `xf:flip-v`
      turn the box, or with no box transform the layer at once. The centre is
      snapped to the half-pixel grid so that every pixel lands on a pixel
      centre: an exact copy, tested.
    - A box takes the pointer, the keys, `tool:commit` / `tool:cancel` and the
      overlay.
    - Another command, undo, switching or closing the document drops it.
- **UI**
  - Edit ▸ Free Transform and the Transform submenu are enabled with the
    `xf:` ids.
  - The `_transform` option bar is shown while a box is up, driven by the new
    `transform_box` message: Interpolation, Warp, ✓, ✗. Interpolation changes
    apply to the box that is up.
  - `actions.js`: in the app, `img:` / `xf:` / `sel:` / `filter:` no longer
    fall through to the mock's "not implemented" toast. This was a T02
    regression for the Image ▸ Rotate items.
- `docs/PROTOCOL.md`: `transform_box`, the `xf:` actions, the `transform`
  command.

## Deviations from the task card

- `preview.rs` shared with M4-T05's filter preview: the transform preview is
  its own module (`transform_preview.rs`). The two share the snapshot
  mechanism (`preview_rev`), not the geometry (a filter keeps the layer's
  size, a transform does not).
- The preview shows a layer's **linked mask** switched off while the box is
  up. The command transforms it.
- Warp's "drag the grid inside" pulls the control points by their Bernstein
  weight at the pressed point (the strongest one fully). Photoshop's exact
  falloff is not documented.
- Numeric X / Y / W / H / angle fields in the option bar: T09.
- The option bar shows the box's size and angle in the status line
  (`tool_info`), not in fields yet.
