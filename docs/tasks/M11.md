## M11-T00 — Decisions (Rob)

1. **PatchMatch** (Barnes et al. 2009, with the multi-scale EM of Wexler et
   al. — what Content-Aware Fill is built on). Fotox's own implementation in
   `fx-ops`:
   - a nearest-neighbour field over a pyramid;
   - the image data is read tile by tile inside a **region of interest**: the
     hole plus the sampling area, clamped by a pixel budget;
   - beyond that budget, it works on a mip level and upsamples the
     correspondence.
   **Yes.**
2. **Liquify**:
   - a displacement field at ¼ resolution (a mesh of 4-px cells), stored in
     the dialog's state and applied through the M6-T01 sampler as a mesh
     mapping;
   - the preview renders the visible tiles at the view level;
   - no Face-Aware Liquify (it needs face landmarks: M13 if ever).
   **Yes.**
3. **Puppet Warp**: as-rigid-as-possible deformation (Igarashi et al. 2005)
   on a triangle mesh built from the layer's alpha, with Density (Fewer /
   Normal / More points) and Expansion. Pin Depth orders fold-overs; Rotate is
   Auto or fixed per pin. **Yes.**
4. **Content-Aware Scale**: seam carving (Avidan–Shamir) with a Protect
   channel (M9-T01) and Protect Skin Tones. For big images it runs at a mip
   level and maps the seams back up. **Yes.**
5. **These all resample the pixels** (no Smart Object until M12): repeated use
   loses quality, as in Photoshop on a normal layer (D-055's reasoning).
   **Yes.**

## M11-T01 — PatchMatch core

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/patchmatch/{mod.rs,nnf.rs,vote.rs,pyramid.rs}` (new).

**What to build**
- `fill(source, hole_mask, sampling_mask, params) -> tiles` over the region of
  interest (T00-1).
- Patch size 7.
- Random search plus propagation, alternating scan order, on rayon over rows
  of the NNF.
- EM voting to the next scale, colour adaptation (optional), and seeded
  randomness (reproducible).

**Tests**
- A hole in a periodic texture is filled with that texture (low error against
  the ground truth).
- The same seed gives the same result.
- Memory stays within the budget for a hole in a synthetic 20 000² image
  (tiles read counted).

## M11-T02 — Content-Aware Fill and the Spot Healing Content-Aware type

**Files**
- `crates/fx-core/src/command.rs` (`Command::ContentAwareFill`);
- `crates/fx-engine/src/workspace/caf.rs` (the Edit ▸ Content-Aware Fill
  workspace);
- `crates/fx-ops/src/brush/heal.rs` (the Content-Aware type).

**What to build**
- Edit ▸ Content-Aware Fill:
  - the sampling area is an overlay the user paints (add / subtract), Auto by
    default: a band around the selection;
  - Colour Adaptation, Rotation Adaptation (None…Full: patches may rotate),
    Scale, Mirror;
  - preview on the visible tiles;
  - output to the current layer, a new layer or a duplicate layer.
- Edit ▸ Fill ▸ Content-Aware (the quick version, defaults).
- The Spot Healing Brush's Type: Content-Aware (the default in Photoshop)
  fills the stroke's area by PatchMatch, then blends it like healing.

**Tests**
- Removing a synthetic object from a gradient + texture background leaves no
  visible seam (the error along the boundary is under a threshold).
- The output-to-new-layer option leaves the source untouched.

## M11-T03 — Patch tool

**Files**
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/patch.rs`.

**What to build**
- Draw a selection (lasso-like) or use the current one, then drag it.
- Source mode: the selected area is replaced by the area it was dragged
  to.
- Destination mode: the other way round.
- Normal (the healing blend, D-045) or Content-Aware (PatchMatch within the
  selection, with Structure and Color options).
- Transparent option.

**Tests**: patching a blemish with clean skin texture (synthetic) removes it
and keeps the destination's shading.

## M11-T04 — Content-Aware Move

**Files**
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/content_move.rs`.

**What to build**
- Select, drag, and a transform box on drop (Transform on Drop).
- Mode Move: the object moves and the hole it leaves is filled by PatchMatch.
- Mode Extend: the object is copied at the new place (buildings, hair).
- Structure (how strictly the moved content keeps its patches) and Color
  (colour adaptation). Sample All Layers.

**Tests**: moving a synthetic object on a textured background leaves the old
place filled and blends the new place's edge.

## M11-T05 — Content-Aware Scale

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/seam.rs` (new);
- `crates/fx-core/src/command.rs` (`Command::ContentAwareScale`);
- the M6-T04 box as its UI.

**What to build**
- Edit ▸ Content-Aware Scale: the Free Transform box constrained to scaling.
  Seam carving (T00-4) removes or inserts seams to reach the size, then a
  normal scale covers the rest (Amount 0–100 % mixes the two, as in
  Photoshop).
- Protect (a channel), Protect Skin Tones.
- The preview is at the view level.

**Tests**
- Narrowing an image with two objects on a flat background keeps both
  objects' widths.
- A protected channel is never cut.

## M11-T06 — Liquify

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/liquify.rs` (new: displacement field, brushes);
- `crates/fx-engine/src/workspace/liquify.rs`;
- `ui/js/native/liquify.js`.

**What to build**
- A modal workspace (Filter ▸ Liquify, Shift+Ctrl+X).
- Tools:
  - Forward Warp, Reconstruct, Smooth, Twirl Clockwise (Alt:
    counter-clockwise), Pucker, Bloat, Push Left;
  - Freeze Mask / Thaw Mask;
  - Hand, Zoom.
- Brush: size, density, pressure, rate, stylus pressure.
- Reconstruct All, Restore All, Show Mesh, Show Backdrop.
- Load / save the mesh (Fotox JSON).
- The displacement field is at ¼ resolution (T00-2). The final result is
  applied through the M6-T01 sampler as a job, one history step "Liquify".

**Tests**
- Forward Warp moves a marked point by the expected displacement.
- Reconstruct returns towards the identity.
- A frozen area does not move.
- The final equals the preview at level 0 (within rounding).

## M11-T07 — Puppet Warp

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/puppet.rs` (new: mesh from alpha, ARAP solver);
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/puppet.rs` (a session like M6-T04's box).

**What to build**
- Edit ▸ Puppet Warp on the active layer. The mesh is built from the alpha
  (contour, then a constrained triangulation, Density, Expansion) and shown
  as an overlay.
- Click = a pin, drag = the ARAP solve, Alt = rotate around the pin, Pin
  Depth. Mode: Rigid / Normal / Distort.
- The mapping is a triangle mesh (the M6-T01 warp grid generalised to any
  triangles). The preview is on the visible tiles.

**Tests**
- With two pins a rigid move is a pure translation (pixels equal).
- Rotating around a pin keeps the pin fixed.
- The mesh stays inside the alpha plus Expansion.

## M11-T08 — Perspective Warp

**Files**
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/perspective_warp.rs`;
- `crates/fx-ops/src/resample` (a mesh of quads).

**What to build**
- Layout mode: draw quads on the image's planes; adjacent edges snap
  together.
- Warp mode: move the quad corners; the planes follow as projective
  mappings per quad, continuous across shared edges.
- Automatic straighten (vertical / horizontal) of the quads' edges; Shift+click
  an edge to straighten it.
- Commit resamples as a job.

**Tests**: two quads sharing an edge warp with no crack along it (the sampled
positions agree on the edge).

## M11-T09 — Vanishing Point

**Files**
- `crates/fx-engine/src/workspace/vanishing_point.rs`;
- `crates/fx-ops/src/vp.rs`.

**What to build**
- A modal workspace (Filter ▸ Vanishing Point):
  - Create Plane (4 corners; the grid shows whether the perspective is
    valid), Edit Plane, extend planes from edges (perpendicular planes);
  - Marquee in perspective, the Stamp (clone in perspective, heal on/off),
    Brush in perspective;
  - Paste into a plane (the clipboard maps onto the plane with its homography).
- Output to the layer, or a new layer (Photoshop: render into a new layer is
  the advice).

**Tests**: pasting a square texture into a synthetic plane gives the expected
projective image; cloning along a plane keeps the pattern's perspective.
