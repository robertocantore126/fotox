## M9-T00 — Decisions (Rob)

1. **Alpha channels are document data.** `Document::channels:
   Vec<Channel { name, image (gray, document depth), color, opacity }>`, saved
   in the `.fxd`. A saved selection is a channel. The colour channels (R, G,
   B) are *views* of the composite, not stored. **Yes.**
2. **Quick Selection algorithm**, non-AI:
   - seeded region growing on colour + edge strength, computed at the view's
     mip level for speed, then refined at level 0 on the selection's boundary
     tiles only;
   - "Enhance Edge" = the T05 refine on the result.
   **Yes.**
3. **Select and Mask edge refinement**:
   - a **guided filter** (He et al.) over the edge band, with the image as
     guide;
   - "Decontaminate Colors" estimates the foreground colour in the band
     (a local weighted average of the confident foreground);
   - no closed-form matting (too heavy for 30k).
   **Yes.**
4. **Magnetic Lasso**: live-wire (Dijkstra on a gradient-magnitude cost),
   searched in a window around the pointer at the view level. Anchors are
   placed by Frequency, as in Photoshop. **Yes.**
5. **Notes and counts are document data** (saved in `.fxd`, not printed or
   exported, as in Photoshop). **Yes.**

## M9-T01 — Channels, Save / Load Selection, Quick Mask

**Files**
- `crates/fx-core/src/{document.rs,channel.rs (new)}`;
- `crates/fx-core/src/command.rs` (`SaveSelection`, `LoadSelection`,
  `DeleteChannel`, `DuplicateChannel`, `ChannelProps`);
- `crates/fx-io/src/fxd/manifest.rs`;
- `crates/fx-render/src/program.rs` (showing a channel or Quick Mask as an
  overlay colour);
- `ui/js/native/channels-panel.js` (new; today a mock).

**What to build**
- Select ▸ Save Selection (new channel, or Replace / Add / Subtract / Intersect
  into an existing one). Select ▸ Load Selection (with Invert and the four
  modes). Ctrl+click a channel's thumbnail = load.
- The Channels panel:
  - the composite plus R, G, B (clicking one shows that channel as gray: a
    render-program option, not new pixels), and the alpha channels;
  - thumbnails, eye toggles, drag to the new/delete buttons.
- Quick Mask (Q):
  - the selection becomes a temporary channel painted as a red overlay (50 %,
    Photoshop's default);
  - every paint tool paints it, since it is a gray target like a mask (M5);
  - Q again turns it back into the selection;
  - Quick Mask Options: colour, opacity, masked vs selected areas.

**Tests**
- Save then Load gives the same coverage.
- The four combination modes.
- Channels round-trip through `.fxd`.
- Quick Mask paint then exit = the painted selection.

## M9-T02 — Grow, Similar, Transform Selection

**Files**
- `crates/fx-core/src/command.rs` (`SelectModify::Grow`, `Similar`);
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/transform.rs` (a selection target).

**What to build**
- Grow: the wand's flood, seeded from the selection's boundary pixels, with
  the wand's tolerance (Photoshop uses the Magic Wand's option bar).
  Contiguous.
- Similar: the same, non-contiguous (all pixels within tolerance of any
  selected colour: a colour set built from the selection, quantised).
- Transform Selection: M6-T04's box, whose target is the selection's coverage
  image instead of a layer. It uses the same gestures and preview (the ants of
  the transformed coverage) and one history step, "Transform Selection".

**Tests**
- Grow stops at an edge.
- Similar selects a separate region of the same colour.
- Transform Selection rotates the coverage and leaves pixels untouched.

## M9-T03 — Color Range

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/select/range.rs` (new);
- `crates/fx-core/src/command.rs` (`Command::ColorRange`);
- `ui/js/native/color-range.js` (the dialog, today a mock).

**What to build**
- Select:
  - Sampled Colors (eyedropper + add/subtract samples, Fuzziness, Localized
    Color Clusters with Range);
  - Reds / Yellows / Greens / Cyans / Blues / Magentas;
  - Highlights / Midtones / Shadows (with the Range sliders of CC);
  - Skin Tones (+ Detect Faces: M13);
  - Out of Gamut (uses the proof LUT, M4).
- A soft coverage per pixel, per tile. The preview (Selection / Grayscale /
  Black Matte / White Matte / Quick Mask) is drawn on the visible tiles at the
  view level.
- Invert. The result is a selection (history step "Color Range").

**Tests**
- Sampled red on a hue ramp selects a band whose width grows with fuzziness.
- Highlights pick the bright end.
- Localized clusters drop the far region.

## M9-T04 — Focus Area

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/select/focus.rs`.

**What to build**
- In-focus detection:
  - local sharpness = the energy of a Laplacian of Gaussian at 2–3 scales,
    normalised;
  - Image Noise Level suppresses a noise floor;
  - In-Focus Range thresholds;
  - Soften Edge smooths the result.
- Add / subtract brushes on the result, as in the dialog.
- The output is a selection, a layer mask or a new layer with a mask.

**Tests**: a synthetic image, sharp on the left and blurred on the right,
selects the left half within a few pixels; the noise floor keeps a noisy flat
area out.

## M9-T05 — Select and Mask

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/select/refine.rs` (new: guided filter, decontamination);
- `crates/fx-engine/src/workspace/select_mask.rs` (new: a modal workspace);
- `ui/js/native/select-mask.js`.

**What to build**
- A modal workspace, like Photoshop's.
- View modes: Onion Skin, Marching Ants, Overlay, On Black, On White, Black &
  White, On Layers. Show Edge / Show Original.
- Tools:
  - Quick Selection (T06);
  - Refine Edge Brush (widens the band where it paints);
  - Brush (add / subtract);
  - Lasso;
  - Hand, Zoom.
- Edge Detection: Radius, Smart Radius (the radius adapts to the edge's
  local width).
- Global Refinements: Smooth, Feather, Contrast, Shift Edge. Decontaminate
  Colors with Amount.
- Output: selection, layer mask, new layer, new layer with mask, new document.
- The work runs **only in the edge band** (the selection's boundary dilated by
  the radius). Interior and exterior tiles stay uniform, so a 30k² document
  costs its boundary's length.

**Tests**
- A soft-haired synthetic (a textured foreground on a smooth background) gets
  a band alpha closer to the ground truth than the input selection (mean error
  halved).
- Decontamination removes background tint in the band.
- Shift Edge +50 % grows the selection.

## M9-T06 — Quick Selection tool (W)

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/select/quick.rs`;
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/quick_select.rs`.

**What to build**
- A brush-like tool (size from `[` `]`):
  - each dab adds (Alt: subtracts) the region grown from the seeds under the
    brush (T00-2);
  - the outline updates live;
  - options: Sample All Layers, Enhance Edge, and Select Subject (greyed until
    M13).
- One history step per stroke ("Quick Selection").

**Tests**
- A stroke inside a flat region bounded by a strong edge selects the region
  and stops at the edge.
- Alt subtracts.
- Live = replay.

## M9-T07 — Magnetic Lasso

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/select/livewire.rs`;
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/lasso.rs` (a third kind).

**What to build**
- Live-wire from the last anchor to the pointer (T00-4).
- Options: Width (the search radius), Contrast, Frequency (auto anchors),
  pen pressure = width.
- A click adds an anchor, Backspace removes one, a double-click or Enter
  closes it, Alt switches to the freehand / polygonal lasso on the fly.

**Tests**: tracing near the boundary of a synthetic disc closes on the disc's
edge within 1.5 px everywhere.

## M9-T08 — Color Sampler, Ruler, Note, Count

**Files**
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/{sampler.rs,ruler.rs,note.rs,count.rs}`;
- `crates/fx-core/src/document.rs` (samplers, notes, counts);
- `ui/js/native/info-panel.js`.

**What to build**
- Color Sampler: up to 10 points, whose values are shown live in the Info
  panel (RGB and a second readout mode). The value is 1 px or an average of
  3×3 … 101×101 (option bar), read from the composite on the fly.
- Ruler:
  - distance, angle, W / H in the Info panel and option bar; the protractor
    (Alt drag from an end);
  - Straighten Layer (a Free Transform rotation, then crop, like Photoshop).
- Note tool: notes stored in the document, a Notes panel, show / hide.
- Count tool: counts in groups (colour, size), numbering drawn as overlay,
  saved in the document.

**Tests**
- A sampler's average.
- The ruler's angle.
- Straighten levels a tilted line.
- Notes and counts round-trip through `.fxd`.

## M9-T09 — Perspective Crop

**Files**
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/crop.rs` (a perspective mode);
- `crates/fx-core/src/command.rs` (Crop with a quad).

**What to build**
- Draw four corners, adjust them, with a grid overlay.
- Enter resamples the quad onto a rectangle with a projective mapping
  (M6-T01), with W / H / Resolution from the option bar or from the quad's
  average sides, and the canvas becomes that rectangle.

**Tests**: a synthetic rectangle drawn in perspective comes out as an
axis-aligned rectangle within a pixel.
