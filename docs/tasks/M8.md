# M8 — Painting and retouching tools

> **Fast mode (D-057, `AGENTS.md`).** Write the code. `**Tests**` paragraphs,

> `HARDEN.md`, so skip them now. T00: take the recommendations as fast defaults.

> **Draft by Claude (26 Sep 2026), not approved yet.** Needs M7-T08 (tool
> kinds). Decisions in M8-T00 first.

Goal: every painting and retouching tool of Photoshop's toolbox that is
not a selection, path or content-aware tool, with Photoshop's options, on
the brush engine of M5:
- Paint Bucket and Gradient;
- Blur, Sharpen and Smudge;
- Dodge, Burn and Sponge;
- the Background and Magic erasers;
- Pattern Stamp, History and Art History brushes;
- Color Replacement, Red Eye and the Mixer Brush;
- plus real brush tips (sampled, dynamics), the Brush Settings panel and
  brush presets.

Every brush-like tool is a `DabOp` (M7-T08). What M5 guaranteed holds for
all of them:
- a live stroke equals its replay;
- the work is bounded by the dabs' tiles;
- one history step, named with Photoshop's tool name.

Exit criteria (proposed):

| Id | Scenario | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S24 | Dodge / Burn / Blur / Smudge stroke at 100 % on B3, 300 px brush | same as S13 (pen → pixels latency, M5) |
| S25 | Paint Bucket, contiguous, on a 10 000² layer | ≤ 2 s, UI responsive (job) |
| S26 | Gradient drag on a 10 000² layer at fit | preview ≥ 30 fps; release → final ≤ 5 s |

## M8-T00 — Decisions (Rob)

1. **Brush presets and ABR.** Presets are Fotox JSON; import of Photoshop
   `.abr` covers **sampled tips** (the image of the tip) and the basic
   dynamics. Computed tips and the dual brush / texture sections come later.
   **Yes.**
2. **Formulas that copy Photoshop's** (Dodge / Burn ranges, Sponge, Smudge
   strength, Color Replacement's modes) are not documented by Adobe:
   - the cards use published approximations, marked `VERIFY`;
   - they are compared with Photoshop in M14 (PSD import gives the test
     files).
   **Yes.**
3. **Gradient fill layers** (`LayerKind::GradientFill`) are derived-tile
   layers like shapes (R6), rendered at every level from their parameters.
   Pattern Fill layers join them (T06). **Yes.**
4. **Mixer Brush model** (Photoshop's is not documented): a reservoir colour
   buffer (the tip's size) and a pickup buffer. Wet = how much canvas is
   picked up, Load = reservoir amount, Mix = canvas vs reservoir ratio, Flow as
   usual. Clean / load after each stroke. **Yes, and say "approximation" in the
   report.**
5. **History Brush source**: a **history state** of the same document. The
   History panel gets Photoshop's source column. States are the snapshots
   History already keeps (D-009), so no new storage is needed. **Yes.**

## M8-T01 — Brush tips, dynamics and presets

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/brush/tip.rs` (sampled tips);
- `crates/fx-core/src/stroke.rs` (`BrushParams` gains a tip source and
  dynamics);
- `crates/fx-io/src/abr.rs` (new);
- `ui/js/native/brush-settings.js` (new; the Brush Settings and Brushes
  panels, today mocks).

**What to build**
- Tips:
  - sampled tips (a gray image, scaled by the one-master-tip rule, see the
    memory note "one-slot stamp cache thrashes");
  - Shape Dynamics: size / angle / roundness jitter with pen pressure / tilt /
    fade control;
  - Scattering (scatter, count, count jitter);
  - Transfer (opacity / flow jitter, pressure);
  - Color Dynamics (fg/bg jitter, hue / saturation / brightness jitter, per tip
    or per stroke);
  - Smoothing (Photoshop's pulled-string, the three modes).
- Jitter is **seeded per stroke**, so live = replay.
- Presets: save / load / rename, with a thumbnail stroke drawn by the brush
  engine. `.abr` import of sampled tips (v6+).

**Tests**
- A sampled tip stamps its image.
- Jitter is identical between live and replay (the stroke stores the seed).
- Smoothing keeps a straight line straight.
- An ABR fixture (a small `.abr` made with a free tool, in `tests/data`)
  imports its tips.

## M8-T02 — Paint Bucket, Magic Eraser, Background Eraser

**Files**
- `crates/fx-core/src/command.rs` (`Command::BucketFill`, `Command::MagicErase`);
- `crates/fx-engine/src/tools/kinds.rs` entries;
- `crates/fx-ops/src/brush/ops/background_eraser.rs`.

**What to build**
- Paint Bucket (G), a `ClickTool`:
  - the M5 wand's flood (`fx-ops::flood`: tolerance, contiguous, anti-alias,
    sample all layers), then a fill of the foreground or a pattern (T06) through
    that coverage, with the mode, opacity and Preserve Transparency;
  - limited by the selection;
  - a job, like the wand.
- Magic Eraser, a `ClickTool`: the same flood, then the pixels go
  transparent; on the Background layer it becomes a layer first (Photoshop).
- Background Eraser, a `DabOp` with a source window:
  - sampling: continuous, once, or the background swatch;
  - limits: discontiguous, contiguous, find edges;
  - tolerance and Protect Foreground Colour;
  - erased = alpha × (1 − coverage × match);
  - on the Background it becomes a layer first.

**Tests**
- A bucket fill on a two-colour image fills one region and stops at the
  edge.
- Non-contiguous fills both regions.
- The magic eraser leaves transparency and turns the Background into a layer.
- The background eraser keeps the protected colour.

## M8-T03 — Gradient tool and Gradient Fill layers

**Files**
- `crates/fx-core/src/gradient.rs` (new: stops, interpolation, types);
- `crates/fx-core/src/command.rs` (`Command::FillGradient`, the
  `LayerKind::GradientFill` variant);
- `crates/fx-render/src/gradient.rs` (a tile renderer shared by the command
  and the layer);
- `ui/js/native/gradient-editor.js` (new: the Gradient Editor, today a mock).

**What to build**
- Types: linear, radial, angle, reflected, diamond.
- Stops: colour and opacity stops with midpoints, in 16-bit.
- Options: Reverse, Dither (ordered, like Photoshop's, so 8-bit bands vanish),
  Transparency, the method (Perceptual / Linear / Classic, Photoshop 2021+).
- The tool is a `DragTool`: a live preview on the visible tiles, one command
  at release, through the selection, with mode and opacity.
- Layer ▸ New Fill Layer ▸ Gradient: a derived-tile layer. Its program is
  rendered per requested tile and level like a shape (R6), and it is editable
  from Properties.
- Gradient presets (Photoshop's defaults: foreground to background,
  foreground to transparent, black-white…).

**Tests**
- Linear endpoints exact.
- A radial gradient is symmetric.
- A dithered 8-bit gradient has no two-level bands wider than the dither
  period.
- The fill layer at level 2 equals the level-0 render downsampled within
  2/255.
- Undo.

## M8-T04 — Dodge, Burn, Sponge

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/brush/ops/tone.rs`.

**What to build**
- Dodge / Burn: Range (Shadows / Midtones / Highlights) weights a luminance
  window; Exposure scales the strength; Protect Tones keeps hue and
  saturation (works on luminance with a saturation clamp). `VERIFY`
  formulas: the published approximations, cited in the code.
- Sponge: Saturate / Desaturate with Flow; the Vibrance option protects
  already-saturated and skin-tone pixels (the Vibrance adjustment's weight,
  M4).
- All three are `DabOp`s: 8/16-bit, masks (on a gray target Dodge/Burn
  work on the gray, Sponge does nothing, as in Photoshop).

**Tests**
- Dodge of midtones lifts a 50 % grey more than black or white.
- Protect Tones keeps hue.
- Sponge desaturate reaches gray; vibrance protects a saturated pixel.

## M8-T05 — Blur, Sharpen, Smudge

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/brush/ops/{focus.rs,smudge.rs}`.

**What to build**
- Blur / Sharpen are `DabOp`s with a source window: a small Gaussian (Blur)
  or an unsharp step (Sharpen, with Protect Detail). Strength is how much
  of the result each dab lays down, and repeated dabs accumulate like
  Photoshop's.
- Smudge is a `StatefulDabOp`:
  - a pickup buffer of the tip's size, seeded at the stroke's start (Finger
    Painting: from the foreground);
  - at each dab it is blended into the canvas by Strength and refreshed from
    the canvas;
  - Sample All Layers reads the composite of the visible layers at the dab's
    tiles only.
- Mode for all three (Normal, Darken, Lighten, Hue, Saturation, Color,
  Luminosity).

**Tests**
- Blur flattens a step edge monotonically with strength.
- Sharpen raises edge contrast and leaves a flat area flat.
- Smudge drags a colour along the path and fades with strength < 1.
- Live = replay for all three.

## M8-T06 — Patterns and the Pattern Stamp

**Files**
- `crates/fx-core/src/pattern.rs` (new: a pattern = an RGBA tile set, in the
  document's resources and a user library);
- `crates/fx-ops/src/brush/ops/pattern.rs`;
- `crates/fx-io/src/fxd/manifest.rs` (patterns saved with the document).

**What to build**
- Edit ▸ Define Pattern (from the selection or the whole image).
- A Patterns panel (library, import/export Fotox JSON + PNG; `.pat` import
  later).
- Pattern Stamp, a `DabOp`: the tiled pattern, Aligned or not, and
  Impressionist (jittered samples).
- Edit ▸ Fill ▸ Pattern and the Paint Bucket's pattern source.
- Layer ▸ New Fill Layer ▸ Pattern: a derived-tile layer with scale and
  angle.

**Tests**
- A pattern tiles seamlessly across document tiles.
- Aligned stamping continues the pattern between strokes.
- The fill layer round-trips through `.fxd`.

## M8-T07 — History Brush, Art History Brush

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/brush/ops/history.rs`;
- `crates/fx-core/src/history.rs` (read access to a state's document);
- `ui/js/native/history-panel` (the source column).

**What to build**
- History Brush, a `DabOp` whose source window reads the **same layer in the
  chosen history state** (snapshot, D-009). If the layer did not exist then,
  or its size changed (Photoshop: "the state's canvas size does not match"),
  the stroke is refused with Photoshop's message.
- Art History Brush:
  - each dab spawns strokes around it, their colour sampled from the source
    state;
  - Styles: Tight/Loose Short/Medium/Long, Dab, Tight/Loose Curl;
  - Area and Tolerance;
  - the randomness is seeded, so live = replay.

**Tests**
- Painting with the History Brush from the first state after a Levels puts
  the original pixels back.
- The size-mismatch refusal.
- An Art History stroke is deterministic for a seed.

## M8-T08 — Color Replacement and Red Eye

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/brush/ops/replace.rs`;
- `crates/fx-core/src/command.rs` (`Command::RedEye`).

**What to build**
- Color Replacement, a `DabOp` with a source window:
  - Mode: Hue, Saturation, Color, Luminosity;
  - Sampling: continuous, once, or the background swatch;
  - Limits and Tolerance as for the Background Eraser;
  - it blends the foreground in by that mode where a pixel matches the
    sample.
- Red Eye, a `ClickTool` → `Command::RedEye { point, pupil_size, darken }`:
  - find the red-dominant blob around the click (a flood on redness
    `r − max(g, b)`, limited to a radius);
  - desaturate it and darken by the amount, feathered at the edge.

**Tests**
- Replacing a red area's hue with blue keeps its luminosity.
- Red Eye turns a synthetic red pupil dark and leaves the iris.

## M8-T09 — Mixer Brush

**Files**
- `crates/fx-ops/src/brush/ops/mixer.rs`.

**What to build**
- A `StatefulDabOp` (T00-4):
  - reservoir, pickup, Wet / Load / Mix / Flow;
  - Load Brush after each stroke, Clean Brush after each stroke;
  - Sample All Layers;
  - the preset combinations of Photoshop's menu (Dry, Moist, Wet, Very Wet…).

**Tests**
- Dry with a loaded reservoir paints the reservoir colour.
- Very Wet on a two-colour field mixes them along the path.
- Clean after each stroke resets.
